The eval's real Telegram turn can now take two optional inputs, for the blind interviews' H product. One is a line added to the end of every request's system prompt (the label line). The other is a list of later owner messages answered in the same chat (the debrief), each its own turn with its own start, reply, outcome and call count. The job's reply and outcome stay the first message's. Without either input, a replay-eval turn runs and reports exactly as before.
