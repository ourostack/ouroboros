- Every phone utterance now ends with a recorded outcome. Each thing a caller says, on a Media Stream or through a Twilio recording, is recorded before its turn runs. Its speech, and any tool that messages a person, is noted before it goes out, and its turn records it as answered, silent (the agent had nothing to say) or failed. A Media Stream reply counts as answered only once its audio went down the call: one cut off by a hangup, or by the caller talking over it before any of it played, is recorded as failed. Live audio is never replayed: an utterance a stopped process left is recorded as failed once the voice sense is listening again, and when it shuts down, and the record says what the caller had already heard. A bridge that cannot take its port leaves the running one's records alone. A recording callback Twilio sends twice runs one turn: a retry while the first is still being answered gets the same reply, and one after it is sent on to listen. A record that cannot be written is logged and never costs the caller the answer or stops the voice sense.
