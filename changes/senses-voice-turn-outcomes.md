- Phone utterances in the cascade voice path (Twilio Media Stream and Twilio recordings) now end with a recorded outcome; the OpenAI Realtime and SIP engines are not covered yet. Each thing a caller says is recorded before its turn runs. Its speech, and any tool that messages a person, is noted before it goes out, and its turn records it as answered, silent (the agent had nothing to say) or failed. A reply counts as answered only once it could reach the caller: a Media Stream reply once some of its audio went down the call (so a hangup or talk-over after part of it played still counts as answered), a buffered recording's reply once its playback files are written, and a streamed recording's reply once its speech is made, before Twilio fetches the stream. A Media Stream reply cut off before any audio played is recorded as failed, saying why: the call ended, the caller spoke again, a tool's audio took its place, or the reply made no audio to send. Live audio is never replayed: an utterance a stopped process left is recorded as failed once the voice sense is listening again, and when it shuts down, and the record says what the caller had already heard. A bridge that cannot take its port leaves the running one's records alone. A recording callback Twilio sends twice runs one turn: a retry while the first is still being answered gets the same reply, a retry of a streamed reply gets the same play link while its stream is still served, and any other retry is sent on to listen. A record that cannot be written is logged and never costs the caller the answer or stops the voice sense.
