- Every phone utterance now ends with a recorded outcome. Each thing a caller says, on a Media Stream or through a Twilio recording, is recorded before its turn runs. Its speech, and any tool that messages a person, is noted before it goes out, and its turn records it as answered, silent or failed. Live audio is never replayed: an utterance a stopped process left is recorded as failed when the voice sense starts or shuts down, and the record says what the caller had already heard. A recording callback Twilio sends twice is answered only once.
