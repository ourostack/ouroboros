- iMessages sent while BlueBubbles or the Mac was down now reach the agent after recovery. iMessage delivers the messages it held minutes after BlueBubbles answers again, dated when they were sent, and BlueBubbles sends no webhook for them, so one catch-up at the moment of recovery missed them. For 30 minutes after an outage every catch-up now keeps reaching back 24 hours, and outage catch-ups are logged.
- A habit whose run was lost before it finished (a crash or restart mid-run) no longer stops for good. Every later firing reused the lost run's occurrence and was refused as its duplicate, which stopped Slugger's heartbeat for 14 hours. A habit a whole interval past due now gets a new occurrence, and the overdue habit and each refused duplicate are logged as warnings.
- launchd now runs interval habits on schedule. The scheduler reloaded every habit's launch agent on its 5-minute pass, which restarted the launch agent's 30-minute clock each time, so launchd never ran the heartbeat. A loaded launch agent whose plist has not changed is now left alone.
- Self-update no longer rolls back a release because one sense is down. `ouro up` refuses to finish while any sense is unhealthy, so a BlueBubbles outage rolled back and blacklisted every release that arrived during it. The updater now accepts a release that answers when everything still wrong was already wrong before the update, and a failed `ouro up` reports its reason instead of an empty one.
