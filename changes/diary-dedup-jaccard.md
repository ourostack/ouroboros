`diary_write` no longer drops a new entry because a short old entry shares a few of its words. Its near-duplicate check now divides the words two entries share by all their distinct words (Jaccard). Before, it divided by the smaller entry's words, so a 3-word entry matched any long entry that contained two of its words. When the tool does skip an entry, it now says "not saved" and names the earlier entry that already says it, instead of "saved diary entry (added=0, skipped=1)".
