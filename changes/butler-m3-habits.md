The Butler's scheduled house check (`sanctuary-health`) and the readiness check for its house and Usenet events now come from the Butler's product registration, so the daemon no longer calls Butler code for them. On the Butler's image they behave as before. Only Supercronic can fire the house check on its cron schedule, and a cron poke without that proof is still refused, now with the message `cron habit 'sanctuary-health' requires authenticated Supercronic provenance`. The proof itself, scheduler attestation, is now part of the harness, and its one-use claims are kept under `state/scheduler/fire-claims` instead of `state/scheduler/sanctuary-fire-claims`. Agents no product runs are unaffected: their habits and events go the usual route and wait for no Butler credentials.
