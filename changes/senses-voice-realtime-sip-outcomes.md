- Every phone utterance now ends with a recorded outcome, on every voice engine: the OpenAI Realtime and SIP engines are covered as well as the cascade path, so the earlier note's "the OpenAI Realtime and SIP engines are not covered yet" no longer holds. On those engines, each thing a caller says is recorded once its transcript arrives, before a reply is asked for. A reply's speech is noted before its audio goes out, and a tool that may message a person is noted before it runs. The next reply the caller asked for answers everything they said since the last one. A greeting or a holding phrase answers nobody, and a reply that only called a tool hands what it answers on to the follow-up. A reply counts as answered only once its audio played: on a Media Stream, once Twilio reports some of it played; on SIP, once OpenAI starts streaming it to the caller. A reply cut off before any audio played is recorded as failed, saying why: the call ended, the caller talked over it, or a tool's audio took its place. On SIP the reply keeps playing when the caller talks, so only the call ending can cut it off. An outbound SIP call that a voicemail menu answered records what it heard as silent. Live audio is never replayed. An utterance a stopped process left is recorded as failed once the voice sense is listening again.
- A second voice bridge started with the same output folder on another port no longer records a running bridge's unanswered utterances as failed. The bridge that holds the folder writes `utterances/bridge.lock` with its process id. Only that bridge records leftovers as failed, when it starts and when it shuts down. A lock left by a stopped process is taken over.
