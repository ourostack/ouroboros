The Butler now registers itself with the harness as a product. The Unraid image names its module in `container-runtime.json`, and every process in the container loads it. Core learns the Butler's two names (`butler`, and `sanctuary` from before the rename) from that registration instead of importing Butler code, for the A2A port, the saved external events and the Telegram failsafe. Nothing changes for the person: the Butler keeps its port and its saved events under either name.
