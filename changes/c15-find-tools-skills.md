`find_tools` now searches the agent's skills as well as its held-back tools. When a skill documents the job (for example a bundle command run through `shell`), the reply includes that skill's section, where its commands run from and how to open the whole skill, instead of only the closest tools or "no matching tools". A section matches only on its own words, with at least one in its heading or its skill's description. One section per skill and at most two skills, with about 900 characters of skill text in all. Only turns that offer `load_skill` or `read_file` get skill text, so a friend's or guest's turn gets none. On Anthropic the section is sent as text after the tool results instead of being dropped.
