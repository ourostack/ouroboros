- Every Teams message now ends with a recorded outcome. It is written to a journal before its turn runs, and its turn's sends, both replies and tools that message a person, are noted before they go out. A message whose process died mid-turn is answered by the next start with a proactive send, but only if nothing went out and it has had fewer than three tries. Otherwise it is recorded as failed, so a reply that already went out is never sent again.
