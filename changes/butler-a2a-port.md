- The Butler keeps its A2A port, 18940, when it runs as `butler`. The default port was worked out from the agent's name, so the image that runs as `butler` served A2A on 18972, and peers that had saved its agent card at 18940 could not reach it. The rollback rehearsal of the rename caught this: the replay gate could not reach the Butler's card. Every other agent's port is unchanged.
