When the channel you hear your agent on (iMessage, Telegram or Teams) has been down for more than 15 minutes, the daemon now tells you once over a channel that still works: the agent's other healthy owner channel first, then mail from the agent's own mailbox to your address. You get one more message once the channel has stayed back for two minutes. It sends at most one alert per channel an hour, treats a channel that flaps as one outage, doesn't count time the Mac was asleep, and keeps its state in the bundle so a daemon restart doesn't repeat it. Mail goes to the `email-address` id on your own friend record, else to the owner of the agent's delegated mail source; with neither, the daemon logs `daemon.owner_outage_alert_undelivered`. A Telegram owner notice now reports whether it was sent or held, so a held alert falls through to mail.
