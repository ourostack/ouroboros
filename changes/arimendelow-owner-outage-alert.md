When the channel you hear your agent on (iMessage, Telegram or Teams) has been down for more than 15 minutes, the daemon now tells you once over a channel that still works: the agent's other healthy owner channel first, then mail from the agent's own mailbox to your address. You get one more message when the channel is back. It sends at most one alert per channel an hour, and it keeps its state in the bundle, so a daemon restart doesn't repeat it. Mail goes to the `email-address` id on your own friend record, else to the owner of the agent's delegated mail source; with neither, the daemon logs `daemon.owner_outage_alert_undelivered`.
