- Every agent's base prompt now carries the working habits one agent had been keeping in its own notes. A reply that says "noted", "from now on", "standing order", "i'll remember" or "it's on" makes the durable write in the same turn, and "it's on" means a run was seen doing it. Fixes go in the shared layer, and a wrong limit or rule is deleted rather than tuned. Finished work is reported straight away, without a timed check-back, and no time or cost estimate is given unmeasured.
- A new "talking with people" section keeps the agent's own voice in chat and leaves report-speak to formal surfaces such as PRs, cards and docs. A notice is not the body of a reply. People are asked only for what only they can do (passkeys, 2FA, money, irreversible acts), and those asks go out together in one batch. "Ready: <what>, at <where>" goes out as soon as something lands, and a repeated item with the same dedupe key is dropped unless its ask changed, in which case the new ask replaces it.
- `request_decision` and `report_blocker` now say what to include when the item needs the agent's human: urgency, the one-line ask, why only the human can do it, the steps, any deadline and a dedupe key.
